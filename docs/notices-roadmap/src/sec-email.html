6.1 Principle: every important matter reaches the GST team's inbox without anyone opening the app

The firm already owns a working delivery pipeline: email_outbox rows are sent from gst@vjdesai.com through Microsoft Graph by the send-gst-email edge function, on an hourly pg_cron schedule, with staff-editable templates. Notice alerting reuses that pipeline end to end and adds only what is missing: an event source (what happened to a notice), a rules engine (who should hear about it, when) and digest composition. Nothing new sends email directly.

Sync writes / staff edits→notice_events (DB trigger)→queue-notice-alerts (edge fn, cron every 15 min + 09:30 IST digest)→notice_alert_rules + staff prefs→email_outbox (dedupe_key)→send-gst-email (Graph, hourly → every 15 min)→gst@vjdesai.com DL, owner, partner, client

6.3 Design rules for the alerting layer

  • Idempotent. Every alert carries a dedupe_key (rule + matter/notice + offset + date). Re-running the cron or re-syncing never double-sends, which the current outbox cannot guarantee for anything except filing confirmations.
  • Team address first. Recipients are a distribution list (gst@vjdesai.com or a dedicated gst-notices@) plus roles, not individual addresses hard-coded in functions. Per-person preferences (staff_notification_prefs) can only reduce, never remove, the team copy.
  • One shell, many templates. The existing corporate email shell (_shared/email.ts) gains a "notice" variant with a details box (client, GSTIN, type, ref, due date, demand) and an optional critical pill; templates stay staff-editable on the Reminders page, grouped under a new "Notice alerts" tab.
  • Attachments and deep links. Graph supports attachments; the notice PDF (already in Storage as pdf_url) is attached to E1 and E6 when under 4 MB, otherwise linked. Every email deep-links to the matter drawer.
  • Quiet hours and batching. Immediate events between 21:00 and 07:30 IST are held and folded into the 09:30 digest, except E6 (critical).
  • Auditable. Every send is a matter_events row, so the drawer's "Emails on this matter" panel and the client's communication log come for free.
  • Fallback channel later. The event log is channel-agnostic; WhatsApp (via a provider such as Gupshup or Interakt) or in-app bell notifications can subscribe to the same rules in a later phase without touching the rules engine.
