5.1 Design principles for the new page

  1. Lead with action, not counts. The first screen answers "what must the team do today" with real notices, owners and deadlines. Counts become context around that queue, never the whole page.
  2. Every number must be defensible. A tile is shown only if its definition is written down (in a tooltip and in this document) and its drill-down list contains exactly the rows it counted.
  3. Money on the page. Demand under dispute, pre-deposits paid and exposure by client are the numbers a partner manages by; they come from columns the schema already has.
  4. One visual language. Use the firm's existing tokens (navy primary, coral accent, success/warning/info/destructive) at the app's standard sizes: body 14px, captions 12px, KPI values 30px, 8-point spacing. No 10–11px text.
  1. Two-week horizon. Deadlines are shown as the next 14 days with counts per day and hearings marked; the month grid moves behind a toggle.
  2. Health, not noise. Sync status, failed logins and email delivery live in one compact "Sync & alerts" card with a last-synced time, instead of a KPI tile that mixes operations with legal metrics.
  3. Client onboarding leaves. Add / Import / Template belong on the Clients page; the dashboard keeps only a search box and a "Sync All" button.
  4. Accessible by default. Every icon button has a label, every clickable cell is a real link or button, focus rings are visible, and colour is never the only signal (chips carry text).

5.3 Visual system

  • Type: Poppins 600/700 for titles and KPI values, Inter for everything else. Sizes: page title 22px, card title 14px, body 13–14px, captions 12px, KPI 30px tabular.
  • Colour semantics (app-wide rule kept): destructive = overdue / critical, warning = due soon / open, info = new / awaiting, success = closed / healthy, navy = neutral primary. Chips always carry text ("−2", "High", "Awaiting client data").
  • Cards: 12px radius, 1px border, no drop shadows; a 4px coloured left rail on the attention tiles only.
  • Density: table rows 40px; 8-point spacing; the page fills the width at 1600–1920px and stacks to one column below 1024px; on a phone the work queue becomes a card list.
  • Empty and error states: every card has a real empty state ("No hearings in the next 14 days") and an error state with a retry, using the shared TableEmptyState.

5.4 The matter drawer (the page behind every row)

Clicking any notice opens a right-hand drawer (or a full page on small screens) that holds the whole matter: title, badges (priority, stage, owner, reviewer, section), a stage stepper with dates and the next statutory step, four fact tiles (demand breakdown, paid / pre-deposit, statutory deadline with its basis, client-data status), a warning banner when a deadline has passed, and tabs for Activity, Reply workspace, Documents, Demand & payments, Emails and Notes. The right column shows the client card, documents (portal PDFs and uploads together), key dates and every email sent on the matter. Every action available in bulk on the queue is available here on one item.
