The office agent logs these clients in on its own PC; their CAPTCHAs come to the CAPTCHA wall in GST Keeper (Notices → Autopilot) for anyone to type.

      
For each client: logs in to the GST portal (you type the CAPTCHA), reads
        notices & orders with their case folders and PDFs, then refund applications, then DRC-03
        payments. Clients are taken most urgent first. Keep the portal tab open until it says done.

Ledger pull: logs in, then reads the credit ledger (opening balance and entries, for
        GST Receivable Reco), the credit reversal & re-claim, liability and cash ledgers for the period,
        and goes on through notices, refunds, DRC-03, taxpayer profile and challans without a run-ledger
        record. It does not write filing status.
